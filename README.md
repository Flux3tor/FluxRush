# FluxRush

just a stupidly hard endless platformer i made in godot

you keep running, jump over shit, collect shards, and try to get as far as possible.

visit https://flux3tor.itch.io/FluxRush to play!

## controls

- `A / D` - move
- `SPACE` - jump
- `R` - restart

## stuff

- endless randomly generated world
- platforms with different heights
- gaps
- spikes
- shards to collect
- distance counter
- random runs every time
- pixel art

## made with

- Godot 4
- GDScript
- Kenney Pixel Platformer

## assets

Most of the game's art comes from Kenney's Pixel Platformer pack.

https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer

It's CC0, so yeah, it's free to use.

## goal

go as far as you can without dying.

that's literally it lol

## running it

open `project.godot` in Godot 4 and press play.

have fun :)
